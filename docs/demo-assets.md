# Demonstration assets

The six short clips generated by `tools/create-demo.mjs` are **synthetic demonstration media**, each 20 seconds long with a still image and silent audio. Their titles are design labels, not claims about real films. The interface labels demo libraries and demo items explicitly.

Photographs are downloaded as local assets from Unsplash's image CDN. Sources were retrieved on 2026-10-12 under the [Unsplash license](https://unsplash.com/license). They are illustrative artwork, not official film posters.
